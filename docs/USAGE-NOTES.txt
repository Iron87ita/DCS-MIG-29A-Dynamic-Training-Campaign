MiG-29A FULCRUM / OPERATIONAL CONVERSION COURSE / EN v1.1

All current campaign titles, briefings, exercise tasking, F10 menus and new artwork are in English. Earlier v1.0 files are retained separately.

USE THE UPDATED .MIZ
Open MiG29A_FF_Operational_Conversion_EN_v1.1.miz in DCS with the MiG-29A Fulcrum Full Fidelity module and Caucasus terrain. The cover and all eight kneeboard pages are embedded: no separate kneeboard installation is required. Use your assigned Show/Hide Kneeboard and next/previous kneeboard page controls in DCS. Do not also install the PNG pack for this mission, or pages may appear twice.

Four HOT Client positions and the original nine dynamic exercises are retained. Rearm before departure; activate an exercise through F10 when ready airborne. Contacts spawn immediately. Read the mission briefing and the Mission Card first.

DELIVERABLES
- Updated .miz with English text, cover and eight 1536x2048 kneeboard pages.
- English kneeboard PDF: the same eight cards for printing or reference.
- PNG ZIP: optional loose pages for a separate personal kneeboard setup.
- English cover PNG: original generated image, copied without recompression.
- ImageGen prompt log: generation and final English localization prompts; built-in tool used.

KNEEBOARD CONTENTS
01 Mission card and R-862 frequencies.
02 Phase 1: air-defence exercises 01-04.
03 Phase 2: ground-attack exercises 05-07.
04 Phase 3: combined exercises 08-09.
05 Air combat system reminders.
06 Initial loadout and navigation reminders.
07 Eight-range schematic, based on actual mission coordinates.
08 F10 commands, completion and training records.

The range diagram uses DCS local grid axes and distances from the first Client start. It is not a terrain chart, geographic bearing solution or substitute for live F10 coordinates. Air scenario ranges and altitudes describe initial contact generation, not mandatory flight profiles. Cockpit channel labels are 0-19; CH0 and CH1 are both Kobuleti in this mission. No tanker or guided air-to-ground capability is added.

Menus remain shared at flight-group level, as in the original. Record queries and START NEXT use the first active player in that group; kill and completion records are stored by player name. SESSION ONLY is the normal sandbox fallback. No host configuration files have been changed.

VALIDATION
ZIP/resource links, four FF Client slots, Lua compilation and unchanged scenario geometry/weather/triggers are checked. Rendered kneeboard pages are visually inspected before delivery. The campaign engine is also checked with simulated DCS APIs; that does not replace in-simulator testing of the cockpit, artwork display or AI. No in-flight validation is claimed.
